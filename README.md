Cooper Standard Holdings is a global automotive supplier headquartered in Northville, Michigan, that designs, engineers, manufactures, and sells sealing systems, fuel and brake delivery systems, and fluid transfer systems for global automotive original equipment manufacturers including Ford, General Motors, Stellantis, and Volkswagen. The company operates in approximately 20 countries with around 32,000 employees and is publicly traded on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker CPS. Cooper Standard does not publish a public developer program or REST APIs. Its digital presence centers on corporate, investor, supplier, and careers websites.
